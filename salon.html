Questions

Good to know

How much is the deposit?

A small part of the price, shown before you pay. It comes off your final bill, and you can also choose to pay in full.

How do I pay the rest?

At the branch when you check in, by cash, bank transfer, POS or card. The front desk sees what you have already paid.

What if I cannot make it?

Cancel from your Hairlux account within the cancellation window and your payment goes back to your Hairlux wallet, ready for your next visit. See our refund policy.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just enter your name, phone number and email. We set up your Hairlux account for you and email a link to set a password, so you can see your bookings any time.

Can I book more than one service?

Yes. Choose as many services as you like for the same visit. We only show times that leave enough room for all of them.

What do I bring to the salon?

Just your reservation code or the QR code from your confirmation email.
