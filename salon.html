Questions

Good to know

How much is the deposit?

A small part of the price, shown before you pay. It comes off your final bill, and you can also choose to pay in full.

How do I pay the rest?

At the branch when you check in, by cash, bank transfer, POS or card.

What if I cannot make it?

Cancel from your Hairlux account within the cancellation window and your payment goes back to your Hairlux wallet for your next visit. See our refund policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Just your name, phone number and email. We set up your account for you so you can see your bookings any time.

Can I book more than one service?

Yes. Choose as many as you like for the same visit. We only show times with room for all of them.
