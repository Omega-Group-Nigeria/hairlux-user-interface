Refund & Cancellation Policy

This page summarizes Hairlux's refund and cancellation terms. It forms part of, and should be read alongside, our full Terms of Service & Booking Policy.

Cancellation Policy

  • Full refund applies only if cancellation is made at least 24 hours before your appointment
  • Cancellations within 24 hours attract a 20% deduction
  • If cancellation occurs after the technician arrives, a 50% charge applies
  • "No-shows" (no communication) are charged 50% of booking value

Rescheduling

  • Clients may reschedule appointments with reasonable notice
  • Hairlux reserves the right to approve or decline rescheduling based on availability

Payment & Refund Processing

Full payment is required upfront to secure all bookings. In special cases, part-payment may be accepted, but the full balance must be completed before service begins. Where a refund is due under this policy, it will be processed back to your original payment method.

Safety-Related Cancellations

The safety of our personnel is a top priority. Hairlux reserves the right to decline or cancel a booking, or withdraw service without refund, if the environment is deemed unsafe, hostile, or presents any security risk.
