Privacy Policy

Hairlux App  ·  Effective 12 September 2026  ·  Last updated 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hairlux (“Hairlux”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use:

  • the Hairlux mobile application for Android and iOS (the “App”)
  • the Hairlux website at https://www.hairlux.com.ng (the “Website”)
  • related booking, wallet, shop, chat, and support services (together, the “Services”)

Hairlux is a beauty and wellness platform in Nigeria. You can book salon and home services, pay for bookings, keep a wallet balance, buy from our shop, message or call your assigned beautician, and get support.

By creating an account or using the Services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

This policy is written to match how the App actually works and to support Google Play Data safety and Apple App Store disclosures. It is not legal advice.

Controller: Hairlux

Website: https://www.hairlux.com.ng

Contact

  • Email: support@hairlux.com.ng

If you have questions about this policy, your data, or account deletion, use the contacts above or the in-app control in section 10.

2. Scope

This policy covers personal data we process as a business operating in Nigeria, including data processed under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and related regulations.

It applies to customers who create a Hairlux user account. It does not replace the privacy practices of independent beauticians, payment providers, or other third parties, except where we share data with them to run the Services.

3. Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: you give it to us, we collect it automatically from your device, and we receive it from service partners.

3.1 Account and profile

When you register or manage your account we collect:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password (stored in hashed form on our servers; we do not store it in plain text)
  • Referral code, if you enter one
  • Account status and identifiers (for example user ID)
  • Security PIN status (whether you have set an App PIN)

We use email one-time codes (OTP) to verify your account.

3.2 Bookings and addresses

When you book a service we collect:

  • Service type, date, time, and booking status
  • Branch details if you book in-salon
  • Home-service delivery address, including full address and map coordinates (latitude and longitude) when you save or pin a location
  • Assigned beautician details needed to fulfil the booking
  • Arrival confirmation, including a scanned QR code value from your beautician when you confirm they have arrived
  • Cancellation, refund, and complaint details you submit

3.3 Payments and wallet

When you pay or use your wallet we collect:

  • Wallet balance and transaction history
  • Deposit and payment amounts
  • Payment status and reference IDs from our payment partners
  • Refund destination (for example wallet credit)

We do not store your full card number, CVV, or card PIN. Card payments are handled by Paystack and/or Moniepoint on their checkout pages or SDKs.

3.4 Shop

If you buy from the Hairlux shop we collect order details, delivery address, payment references, and fulfilment status.

3.5 Chat and audio calls

For a booking you may message or call your beautician. We process:

  • Chat message content and metadata (sender, time, booking/channel ID)
  • Audio call signalling and call metadata (who called, when, duration)
  • Microphone audio during a live call (processed in real time to connect the call; we do not keep a call recording unless we tell you otherwise)

Chat and calling are provided with Stream (GetStream).

3.6 Location

With your permission, the App accesses approximate and precise location while you use it, to:

  • Show nearby Hairlux branches
  • Help you set or confirm a home-service address on the map
  • Use your current position when you choose “use my location”

During an active home-service booking we may also show live beautician location so you can track arrival. That location belongs to the beautician’s device and is shown to you only for that booking.

You can refuse or later revoke location permission in your device settings. Some features (nearby branches, map pin, live tracking) will not work without it.

3.7 Camera

With your permission, the App uses the camera to scan an arrival QR code from your beautician. The App reads the code value to confirm arrival. It is not used as a general photo gallery or social camera. You can refuse camera permission; you will not be able to scan arrival codes in-app.

3.8 Microphone and Bluetooth

With your permission, the App uses the microphone for audio calls with your beautician. On Android the App may also use Bluetooth permissions so you can take the call on a headset. Audio is used only while a call is active.

3.9 Push notifications

If you allow notifications we collect a device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, and the equivalent token on iOS). We use it to send:

  • Booking updates
  • Wallet credits or debits
  • Chat messages
  • Incoming call alerts
  • Other service notices

You can turn off notifications in device settings. Service SMS or email may still be sent where needed for the booking.

3.10 Biometrics and App PIN

You may set a Hairlux security PIN and optionally unlock the App with Face ID, fingerprint, or another device biometric.

  • The PIN is processed by Hairlux to lock the App.
  • Biometric data (your face or fingerprint template) stays on your device and is handled by the operating system. Hairlux does not collect, store, or upload biometric templates.

3.11 Support

If you contact support we collect:

  • Your name, email, and phone number
  • User ID
  • Booking ID, booking code, and booking status when you open chat from a booking
  • The messages you send, including files you attach

In-app support chat is provided by Crisp. We identify you in Crisp so the conversation can continue across sessions.

3.12 Device and usage data

We automatically collect:

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Approximate network information such as IP address
  • Crash, performance, and diagnostic data
  • App interaction needed to operate features (for example which booking you opened)
  • Local preferences stored on the device (session tokens, last email used to sign in, notification prompt state, selected branch)

We use Expo Updates to deliver app updates. That process may receive device and app-version information required to serve the correct update.

3.13 Website cookies

On the Website we may use cookies or similar technologies to keep a session, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser. The App does not use browser cookies; it uses device storage and the technologies listed above.

3.14 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not ask for your contacts, SMS inbox, or precise advertising ID for ads. We do not sell personal data.

4. How we use information

We use personal data to:

  • Create and secure your account, verify email, and authenticate you
  • Process bookings, assign beauticians, and deliver home or in-salon services
  • Take payment, fund your wallet, issue refunds, and prevent fraud
  • Fulfil shop orders
  • Show maps, nearby branches, and live arrival tracking
  • Enable chat, audio calls, and arrival QR confirmation
  • Send transactional notifications (push, email, or SMS)
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes
  • Operate referrals
  • Improve reliability, fix bugs, and keep the App updated
  • Comply with Nigerian law, tax, accounting, and lawful requests
  • Enforce our terms and protect users, staff, and beauticians

Legal bases (NDPA). We process data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (account, booking, payment), because we have a legitimate interest in security and service improvement that does not override your rights, because you gave permission (location, camera, microphone, notifications, biometrics), or because the law requires it.

5. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We share data only as needed to run the Services:

Beauticians see only what they need for your booking. They are not allowed to use your data for their own marketing.

6. Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack and/or Moniepoint. Those companies handle card data under their own privacy policies. Hairlux receives payment status, amount, and a transaction reference so we can confirm your booking or wallet deposit.

Read:

  • Paystack Privacy Policy
  • Moniepoint’s published privacy policy on their website

8. Data storage, security, and retention

8.1 Storage

Account, booking, wallet, and address data are stored on Hairlux servers and with the processors listed above. Some processors may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where we do that, we take steps appropriate under Nigerian data-protection law.

On your device we store session tokens and preferences in secure or local storage so you stay signed in and the App works offline for basic state.

8.2 Security

We use industry-standard measures such as HTTPS, hashed passwords, access controls, and device secure storage for tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your password, PIN, and device lock private.

8.3 Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, including:

  • While your account is active
  • For bookings, payments, and wallet history needed for support, refunds, and accounting
  • For a limited period after account closure if the law, a dispute, or fraud prevention requires it

When data is no longer needed we delete or irreversibly anonymise it.

Support chats, payment records, and invoices may be kept longer where the law requires.

9. Your rights

Subject to the NDPA and other applicable law, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated data (see section 10)
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (location, camera, microphone, notifications, biometrics) in device settings
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

To exercise these rights, email support@hairlux.com.ng from the address on your account and tell us what you need. We may verify your identity before we act. We will respond within the time required by law.

You can also update name, phone, and addresses in the App where those screens exist.

10. Account and data deletion

Hairlux accounts can be deleted. Logging out or locking the App is not deletion.

10.1 How to delete your account

In the App: open Account → Help and FAQ's → Delete account. You must confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed in with Google only. When deletion succeeds we sign you out and clear the session on that device.

You cannot delete the account while you still have unfinished bookings or shop orders. Complete or cancel them first, then try again.

By email (also available without the App): write to support@hairlux.com.ng with the subject Account deletion and the email or phone number on the account. We will confirm your identity and process the request.

10.2 What we delete or anonymise

When your account is deleted we:

  • Remove or irreversibly anonymise your name, email, phone number, password, App PIN, and Google sign-in identifier
  • Soft-delete saved addresses
  • Revoke your signed-in sessions and device push tokens
  • Send a confirmation email to the address on the account before that address is removed

We keep bookings, payments, and wallet records in anonymised form where we need them for refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, tax, or an unresolved dispute. Those records are no longer tied to your name or contact details. We will tell you if a support request leaves any identifiable data in place and why.

If you also have a separate Hairlux beautician account, deleting the customer account does not delete that beautician account.

10.3 After deletion

You can register again later with the same email, phone, or Google account. That creates a new account; it does not restore the old one.

Beautician-facing copies of a completed booking (for example a past job record they already saw) may not be fully recalled from their device.

11. Children

The Services are for adults. Hairlux does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, and the App is not directed at children.

If you are under 18, use the Services only with a parent or guardian’s involvement where required. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it. Contact support@hairlux.com.ng if you believe a child has created an account.

12. International users

Hairlux is operated from Nigeria. If you access the Services from another country, your information will be processed in Nigeria and in the countries where our processors operate.

13. Third-party links and SDKs

The App and Website may open third-party pages (for example payment checkout, maps, or social profiles). Those sites have their own policies. Hairlux is not responsible for them.

The App includes software development kits (SDKs) from the partners in section 5. Those SDKs may collect device or usage data as described in their policies. We only include SDKs needed to run bookings, payments, maps, chat, calls, notifications, support, and updates. We do not include third-party advertising SDKs for interest-based ads.

14. Automated decisions

We do not use your data for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects (for example automated credit scoring). Fraud and security checks may flag unusual activity for human review.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The new version will be posted at https://www.hairlux.com.ng/privacy/app-privacy-policy.html and in the App, with a new “Last updated” date.

If a change is material we will notify you in the App, by email, or both. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy. If you do not agree, stop using the Services and request account deletion.
