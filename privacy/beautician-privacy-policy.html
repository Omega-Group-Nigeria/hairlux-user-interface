Privacy Policy

Hairlux Beautician  ·  Last updated 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hairlux (“Hairlux”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), operated by Omega Pathway, collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Hairlux Beautician mobile application (the “App”) and related beautician services.

This policy applies to beauticians and other service professionals who create an account in the App. It does not replace the privacy policy for the Hairlux customer app, which is a separate product.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Who we are

Hairlux is a home-service marketplace. The Beautician App lets verified professionals receive job offers, navigate to bookings, complete services, chat or call customers, and receive payouts.

Data controller: Omega Pathway (Hairlux)

App: Hairlux Beautician

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the marketplace, verify professionals, dispatch jobs, support calls, and pay you.

2.1 Information you give us

Category Examples
Account First name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, password, referral code
Profile Bio, profile photo, specialties, years of experience, travel radius, portfolio URL
Identity and onboarding (KYC) Government identity documents, certification images, application / verification video, and any notes you submit for profile review
Banking and payouts Bank name, bank code, account number, account name, withdrawal requests
Support Messages you send through in-app support (including Crisp chat)

You must be at least 18 years old to register. We use date of birth to enforce that rule.

2.2 Information from your device

Category Why we collect it
Location (while you are online) Approximate and precise GPS so we can match you with nearby jobs and show your position on the map
Location (while you are on a job, including background) Precise GPS while a booking is active so the customer can track your arrival and so we can verify en-route / arrived / complete-service actions

Background location. We request background location only so customers can see your progress while you are assigned to an active job, and so arrival checks can still work if the App is not in the foreground. We do not use background location for advertising. You can turn location off in system settings; you may then be unable to go online or complete job steps that require a GPS fix.

2.3 Information created when you use the App

  • Availability status (offline, online, offered, on job)
  • Job offers you receive, accept, or decline
  • Active and historical bookings, including booking code, schedule, services, estimated earnings, and addresses (masked until you are assigned; fuller address when you need to attend)
  • Live tracking points during a job
  • In-app chat messages and call metadata for a booking (provider: Stream)
  • Wallet balance, job earnings, and payout history
  • Support conversation history

2.4 Information from others

  • Customers and Hairlux operations: booking details, service location, and feedback related to a job you accept
  • Identity / KYC vendors: verification results (for example QoreID) when you complete identity checks
  • Banks / payment partners: confirmation that an account number matches a name, and payout status
  • Maps: map tiles and geocoding via Google Maps when we show addresses or navigation

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  1. Create and secure your account (including email OTP and password resets)
  2. Verify that you are eligible to work on Hairlux (age, KYC, certifications, profile review)
  3. Dispatch job offers to nearby, available beauticians
  4. Let you accept or decline work and complete the job lifecycle (en route, arrived, in service, complete)
  5. Share live location with the customer only for an active assigned booking
  6. Enable in-job chat and audio calls
  7. Send transactional notifications (offers, expiry, booking status, chat, incoming calls)
  8. Calculate earnings and process withdrawals to your bank account
  9. Prevent fraud, abuse, and unsafe behaviour
  10. Provide customer support
  11. Maintain, debug, and improve the App (including delivering JavaScript updates over our own update server)
  12. Meet legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory duties

Legal bases we rely on (NDPA): contract (providing the App and paying you), legitimate interests (security, dispatch quality, fraud prevention), consent (optional permissions such as notifications or support chat), and legal obligation where the law requires us to keep records.

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may transfer under this policy or a successor policy we will notify you about.

5. Permissions you control

You can deny or later revoke OS permissions. The App will still open, but some features will not work.

Permission If you refuse
Location You cannot be matched to nearby jobs or complete GPS-gated job steps
Background location Customers may not see live arrival tracking; some on-job features may fail when the App is backgrounded
Camera You cannot capture KYC documents, certifications, or QR verification in-app
Microphone You cannot record application video or take audio calls
Photos You cannot upload existing images from the library
Notifications You will not get offer or chat alerts when the App is closed

Uninstalling the App or using in-App logout / token unregister stops push to that device when the request succeeds.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The mobile App does not use web cookies. We use local storage and secure storage on the device for session tokens, preferences, and similar technical data. The support widget and any in-App web views may set their own cookies; see those vendors’ policies.

7. Advertising

We do not use your data to show third-party advertising in the Beautician App. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-app advertising. We do not require the Advertising ID for our own ads.

8. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it, unless the law requires a longer period.

Typical periods (we may keep data longer for disputes, fraud, or legal holds):

  • Account and profile: for as long as the account is open. If you delete your account in the App, we anonymise identifying fields and mark the account deleted (see section 12)
  • KYC and identity documents: for as long as required for compliance and re-verification
  • Job and tracking history: for operational history, customer disputes, and accounting
  • Chat and call records: for the life of the booking relationship and a limited archive period
  • Payout and wallet records: for tax and financial record-keeping (often several years)
  • Device push tokens: until you log out, uninstall, or the token is replaced
  • Support tickets: until the issue is closed plus a limited archive period

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls on staff tools, and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your password and device lock confidential.

10. International transfers

Our servers, maps, push, chat, KYC, and support vendors may process data outside Nigeria (including the EU, UK, or United States). Where we transfer personal data abroad, we take steps required by Nigerian law, such as contracts and assessing the destination’s protections.

11. Your rights

Subject to the NDPA and other applicable law, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (much of this you can edit in the App)
  • Delete your account from the App (Help & support → Delete account), or ask us by email. See section 12
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (this does not affect prior lawful use)
  • Port data you provided to us, where technically feasible
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

To exercise these rights, use the in-App account tools or contact us using section 16. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within the time the law requires.

12. Account deletion

You can delete your Hairlux Beautician account yourself while signed in:

Account → Help & support → Delete account

You must confirm with your password. Deletion cannot be undone. You will be signed out on this device.

When we will not delete yet

We refuse the request if you still have unfinished work: a booking that is not completed or cancelled, or a job offer you have accepted. Finish or cancel that work, then try again.

What we do when deletion succeeds

  1. Send a confirmation email to the address on the account (before it is removed)
  2. Anonymise your name, email, phone, password, and similar identifiers
  3. Set the account inactive and record a deletion time
  4. Revoke your sign-in sessions and push tokens
  5. Take your beautician profile offline and clear payout / KYC identifiers we no longer need

Bookings and payments stay on file in anonymised form for operations, disputes, tax, and legal duties. A short audit record (that you deleted an account, without your personal details) may be kept.

Deleting a beautician account does not delete a customer account that happens to belong to the same person. Those are separate.

The same email, phone, or Google account can register again later.

If in-App deletion is unavailable (for example a Google-only account with no password), contact us using section 16 and we will handle the request.

13. Children

The App is for professionals aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Automated decisions

Dispatch uses your location, availability, verification status, and operational rules to decide which job offers you see. This is necessary to perform our contract with you. It is not used for advertising. If you believe a dispatch outcome is wrong, contact support.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will change the “Last updated” date and, if the change is material, notify you in the App or by email. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

18. Prominent disclosure (background location)

Hairlux Beautician collects location data to match you with nearby jobs while you are online, and to share your live location with the customer while you are on an assigned job, including when the App is in the background or the screen is off.

This data is used for job dispatch, arrival tracking, and service verification. It is not used for advertising.

You can stop sharing by going offline, completing or leaving the job as the product allows, or turning off location permission in system settings.
